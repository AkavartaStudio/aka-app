Aka — Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

Aka is a personal app that measures how much of your day you spend in real life, and the conditions that make that possible. This page explains what it does with your data, in plain terms.

The short version

  • Aka runs entirely on your own device. There is no server, no account, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • Your data never leaves your iPhone, Apple Watch, or your own iCloud (where Apple Health and Calendar already store it).
  • Nobody at Akavarta — including us — can see your data. We built it, we don't see it.

What Aka reads

With your permission, Aka reads the following, entirely on-device:

  • Apple Health: sleep, step count, exercise and stand minutes, heart rate variability, active and dietary energy, and screen time — used to calculate your daily scores.
  • Calendar: event start/end times, used to count time spent in real-world events. Aka does not read event titles, locations, or attendees beyond what's needed to tell an event happened.

What Aka writes

  • Apple Health: a daily summary of your Aka score, so it shows up alongside your other health data in the Health app.
  • On-device storage: your daily scores and anything you manually log (meals, personal care, space care, connections) are saved locally using Apple's on-device data storage (SwiftData). This is shared only between your own iPhone, Apple Watch, and home screen widget, via Apple's App Group mechanism — still entirely on your own devices, never to a server.

What we don't do

  • We don't run any servers that Aka talks to. There's nothing to hack, leak, or subpoena, because it doesn't exist.
  • We don't use analytics, ad tracking, or any third-party SDK that phones home.
  • We don't sell, share, or have access to your data. We can't — it never reaches us.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything Aka stored on your device. To remove what Aka wrote to Apple Health, go to the Health app → your profile → Apps → Aka → and turn off or delete access.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional server feature — this page will be updated first, and any new data use will require your explicit permission.
